# k-grid fill: lex1dx sig5 vs Grønlund wilber-interp

One warm-up and 3 timed reps; times are medians, in seconds. **Comparison is post-sort only**: every method is given the same array, already sorted with `std::sort` (that sort is timed separately and logged as `sort_s`; it is not added to `post_s`). lex1dx **sig5** starts at 5 significant digits, with the k-preserving digit fallback and group-level Refine (the reported configuration). M = max(4096, 32k): M stays 4096 for k ≤ 128 and grows with k above that, so these cells evaluate the default budget policy rather than isolating k. Exact SSE* is the two-pass long-double SSE of unweighted Grønlund wilber-interp on the sorted points. REE is (SSE − SSE*) / SSE* × 100. Peak RSS is the cell process (`getrusage`).

If a wilber-interp cell timed out or crashed, its row has `status=timeout` or `status=crash` and lex REE is left blank.
