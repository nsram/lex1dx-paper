This folder is the output of packs/bot_v10_rerun_mac_pack.

It is a complete rerun of the v8 scaling grid and the v9 controls, but
with a different sig5 pipeline (raw-data Refine and a k-preserving
digit fallback). The paper reports the group-level Refine path, so the
figures and tables are built from results/mac_out_v8 and
results/mac_out_v9, not from these files. The CSVs are included for
provenance.
